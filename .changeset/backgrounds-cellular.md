The four cellular-automaton backgrounds are drawn: `cyclic` (Cyclic CA), `brian` (Brian's Brain), `daynight` (Day & Night) and `rule110` (Rule 110). Each runs on a seeded grid painted through one off-screen image scaled without smoothing, opens already formed (the reduced-motion still frame), keeps moving without dying out, draws the same under every style and recolours with the palette and mode (MOTIR-11616).
