A · Defaults — every area on the style’s default background (the Wave)

The step gains a fifth field, Backgrounds, below Typography: one chip row per page area, the seven backgrounds and None, each chip leading with a still swatch of its background the way a palette chip leads with its swatch dot. The live preview gains the three areas under the existing example window. Dashed borders mark what is new; they are not part of the design.
