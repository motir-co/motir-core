Grill me before planning — the control outside a run, the grilling in a question-only conversation, and the plan offer held, then shown with what was settled

With Grill me on in a conversation that has no plan yet, an answer that would offer to plan opens a grilling first. The offer is held — no Confirm, no Decline — until the grilling ends by finishing, by Stop or by switching Grill me off, or ends at once because there is nothing to ask. Then the shipped plan offer appears, naming what the answers settled.

Every panel is the overlay’s conversation rail at 480px. The example request is shared export presets; the questions are placeholders for layout. Panels 1–10 are the state sequence; 11 is Chinese, 12 dark.
