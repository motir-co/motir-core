The composed requirement in the left pane — read it before Confirm, and keep it after

A DELTA on plan-offer.mock.html. A no-target plan offer carries the requirement Motir composed from the conversation. The left pane’s whole canvas area becomes a read-only document while the offer is undecided — no panel or card inside the pane — and the rail’s offer panel is unchanged. The spec is design/ai-chat/design-notes.md § The composed requirement in the left pane.

1

Pending — the composed requirement stands in the left pane

A no-target plan offer carries a requirement. While the offer is undecided the left pane shows it as a read-only document: the title, five section headings with their text, and the assumptions as rows with their ids. It replaces the canvas. There is no edit control and no Confirm in the pane — Confirm stays on the offer in the rail, and it is the only go.

5

Confirming and cannot save — the page is written before planning starts

Confirm saves the requirement as a page, then starts planning. While it saves, both buttons are disabled and Confirm reads “Confirming…”. If the page cannot be saved nothing starts: the rail shows one line under the offer, in the danger hue on its tint, with Confirm kept and enabled to try again. This is a different line from “cannot start” — that one replaces the offer; this one keeps it.

Confirmed, declined, reopened — the document is kept as a record
