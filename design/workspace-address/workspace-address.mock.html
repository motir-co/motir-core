Workspace settings › Public address

Where a workspace owner or admin claims, renames or releases the workspace's one name under motir.site — Story MOTIR-4528, subtask MOTIR-9513. The rules every string states are §8 Amendment 3 of the public-tenant-addresses ADR: one name, no aliases; a retired name stops answering at once; a name held under 24 hours returns at once, one held longer is held for 90 days; one change per 30 days. Today is 28 October 2026 in every panel.

The rail and the shell are the shipped ones, composed by citation.
