MY AGENTS, delta — an agent waiting for room — /my-agents?agent=<id>

It amends design/my-agents/my-agents--boot.mock.html (the boot read-out) Panels 1, 2, 7 and 10, and the row menu of design/my-agents/my-agents--panel.mock.html. Both stay records and are not edited. It draws one new step, Waiting for room, shown when there is no machine free for agents right now: for a NEW agent (room anywhere) and for a HIBERNATED agent being woken (room where its files are).

Waiting is never failing. The step is drawn in the in-progress family, never with a danger hue, and a full pool never produces the failed read-out. No other place to run the agent is offered: the agent waits, and starts by itself once there is room. No retry button is drawn, because it retries by itself.

What is new is what the row says when there is no room: the status label reads Waiting in the neutral tone (the muted ground and the to-do dot, the tone Deleting and Hibernating use), and the line under the name is Waiting for room….

A hibernated agent’s Wake (its panel’s face, or the row menu’s Wake) leads into the same step when there is no room where its files are. Its row reads Waiting with Waiting for room to wake…

2CREATE, WAITING · WAKE, WAITING — the two reasons side by side.

The waiting step row is the first row of the read-out, above Machine provisioned. It takes the in-progress row’s ground and ink (--el-tint-sky, --el-text-strong) with an hourglass glyph that does not rotate: under way, nothing failed. Its time column is the step’s ticking clock. The rows after it are waiting, exactly as in the boot read-out.

The reason line sits directly under the label: one or two plain sentences, body weight. The detail line under it says which case applies, in a few words. Neither ever names a provider, a machine type, an account or a limit code.

No machine details yet on a new agent: the meta line is the coding agent and the project only, and the terminal area keeps its booting words. A woken agent’s clone rows are skipped as in any wake.

3WAITING FOR A LONG TIME — the same step, with a since-time.

From ten minutes on, the waiting row gains one quiet line: Waiting for 12 minutes — Motir keeps trying by itself. It is the row’s own ink on its own ground; nothing turns red, nothing blinks, and there is no retry button, because nothing needs pressing. The clock keeps ticking in the time column.

The read-out has no deadline while waiting: the boot deadline starts when the machine is being created, not before. A wait is never turned into a failure by time alone.

4ROOM APPEARED — the waiting step turns done, and the boot carries on.

When room appears the waiting row becomes a done row reading Room found, with how long it waited in its time column; its reason and detail lines go. The next step goes in progress and the boot continues exactly as in the boot read-out: provisioning, starting, cloning (or skipped on a wake), the terminal check, ready. The label returns to Starting / Waking, and the row’s line to the step it is on.

The attempt’s clock in the head counts the whole attempt, the wait included, and the running summary reads Booted in {time} over that same span.

6WAITING IS NOT FAILED — the two side by side.

A genuine failure keeps the boot read-out’s failed look, unchanged: the danger hue in the row’s border and glyph, the reason under it, Failed on the label, and the way out. Waiting is never drawn that way, and a full pool never produces the failed state: no danger hue, no Failed label, no way-out row.

No alternative is offered. The waiting step offers no other place or kind of machine to run on, and no button to try elsewhere: the agent waits, and starts on its own once there is room.
